### 2. The latency TOST lacks the across-restart point estimator and 90% CI construction

The contract freezes each restart value as the median of 50 paired log-ratios, but only says that the “20 restart values’ 90% CI” must lie inside the equivalence bounds. It does not state whether the final center is the mean or median, or whether the CI is Student-t, percentile bootstrap, or another interval. Those choices can change pass/fail at ±2%.

Exact fix: freeze one calculation. The conventional executable choice is:

```text
r_j = median of the 50 paired log(merged/released) values in restart j
estimate = mean(r_1,...,r_20)
s = sample standard deviation of the 20 r_j values (ddof=1)
90% CI = estimate ± t_(0.95,19) * s / sqrt(20)
PASS iff both CI endpoints lie strictly inside [log(.98), log(1.02)]
```

State endpoint strictness explicitly and add the estimator, `ddof`, distribution, degrees of freedom, and quantile to JSON. If a median/bootstrap analysis is intended instead, freeze its exact resampling seed, replicate count, quantiles, and interpolation before G0.
